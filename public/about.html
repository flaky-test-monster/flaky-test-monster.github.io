Where the monster comes from

Born as a joke in a Slack channel, now 3D printed and on exhibition in Vienna.

TL;DR

The Flaky Test Monster started as a joke in the Slack channel of the team that works on Discover, the part of Kibana where you explore your data, around the Kibana 8.11 and 8.12 releases. Somebody had to be making all those flaky tests, so the team gave the culprit a face. It never left. Around Kibana 9.5 it was 3D printed, and in October 2026 it moved into the physical world for good: as an exhibit at the Happylab makerspace’s 20th anniversary in Vienna. This app, flakifier, turns you into one, too.

What is a flaky test?

Software teams check every change with automated tests, run on a CI (continuous integration) system: the quality control of software engineering. A test is flaky when it sometimes passes and sometimes fails on the same code. Nothing changed, yet the result did. Typical causes are timing (the test clicks a button before the page has finished loading), tests that depend on each other’s leftovers, a slow network or a busy CI machine.

A flaky test rarely says why it failed. Rerun it and it passes. At a certain scale every CI setup has some; scale up a bit further and they are part of daily life: failures to investigate, tests to skip and unskip, releases held up by a red build that turns green on retry. Eventually you start wondering whether your CI pipeline is haunted… or whether a little goblin somewhere is handcrafting flaky tests with love.

Passed locally, failed on CI.

Timeline

  1. 27 October 2023 · Kibana 8.11 / 8.12

    A suspect in the Slack channel

    A teammate notices that while a colleague was on holiday, no new flaky tests came in, and now that they are back… starting to think you might be the flaky test monster. Less than an hour later, an AI-generated picture of the culprit lands in the channel: Caught in the act! The monster has a face.

  2. Wanted: 8.12 or 8.11

    From pixels to plastic

    The joke stays. The monster gets a wanted poster, skipped or unskipped, and then a body: green, square, with spiky black hair, googly eyes and a mouth full of teeth, printed layer by layer.

  3. On tour

    The monster finds friends and travels

    Together with a little robot chef and a crocheted red ball, the monster sees the sea, the mountains and a sunset. Flaky tests travel well.

  4. More figures

    The team, in resin

    The monster gets company from the people it keeps busy. GitHub avatars turn into resin figures, and teammates turn into garden gnomes.

  5. Vienna

    Hasta la vista, flaky test

    Some tests are hard to kill. The monster gets a leather jacket, a red eye and a minigun, and a Terminator turns up at a Vienna U3 station.

  6. The team

    Everybody gets the eyes

    By now the monster is part of the team, and the team is a little bit monster.

  7. October 2026 · Kibana 9.5

    The monster moves into Happylab

    On 9 October the goblin from the CI pipeline entered the physical world, as one of the makers’ exhibits at the 20th anniversary of the Happylab makerspace in Vienna. Last Saturday was the party. Maybe the monster stays at Happylab, and that solves the flaky test problem?

  8. Kibana 9.6+

    Next

    Still very much alive, and looking forward to all the exciting new opportunities to make tests flaky. Meanwhile, turn yourself into a monster or help it find a country for the Eurovision Song Contest.
