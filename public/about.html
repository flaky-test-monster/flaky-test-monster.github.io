Where the monster comes from

Born as a joke in a chat, 3D printed, traveling the world.

TL;DR

The Flaky Test Monster started as a joke in a chat of the team that works on Discover, the part of Elastic’s Kibana where you explore your data, around the Kibana 8.11 and 8.12 releases. Somebody had to be making all those flaky tests, so the team gave the culprit a face. Every measure to get rid of it failed: sending it on holiday to Italy, Croatia, Austria, Spain and Las Vegas, finding it a job on LinkedIn, giving it away as prints and buttons to other teams. Around Kibana 9.5 it was 3D printed, and in October 2026 it moved into the physical world for good: as an exhibit at the Happylab makerspace’s 20th anniversary in Vienna.

What is a flaky test?

Software teams check every change with automated tests, run on a CI (continuous integration) system: the quality control of software engineering. A test is flaky when it usually passes and sometimes fails. Nothing changed, yet the result did. Typical causes are timing, tests that depend on each other’s leftovers, a slow network or a busy CI machine.

A flaky test rarely says why it failed. Rerun it and it passes. At a certain scale every CI setup has some; scale up a bit further and they are part of daily life: failures to investigate, tests to skip and unskip, releases held up by a red build that turns green on retry. Eventually you start wondering whether your CI pipeline is haunted… or whether a little goblin somewhere is handcrafting flaky tests with love.

Passed locally, failed on CI.

Timeline

  1. 27 October 2023 · Kibana 8.11 / 8.12

    A suspect in the chat

    A teammate notices that while kertal was on holiday, fewer flaky tests came in. Suspicious: is kertal the flaky test monster? kertal asked an AI what a flaky test monster looks like. There were similarities in the picture it returned, no question, but when the monster was turned into a 3D print two years later, it was clear: the monster was its own entity.

  2. On tour

    Sent on holiday

    Once the monster was a physical entity, kertal took it on holiday, trying to convince it to stay in Italy, Croatia, the Austrian Alps, Spain or Las Vegas, together with the monster fighters Minichef and Knitty McGritty. That didn’t work so well: the flaky test problem persisted.

  3. 20 July 2025 · Kibana issue #228706

    A flaky postcard from Italy and Austria

    The monster opened an issue of its own in the Kibana repository, labeled test-failure-flaky. kertal had talked it into a holiday in Italy and the Austrian Alps: a break is important, to come back with great new ideas for making the CI pipeline flaky. On the way to the post office, to send the team a postcard, the monster passed out, and woke up with an arm missing. Had kertal only invited it to get rid of the good old Flaky Test Monster? Would it be replaced by a soulless AI agent that makes flakiness that is easy to fix? Has @kertal become evil? HELP!

    The team was not very sympathetic: one teammate formally offered it to resume vacationing indefinitely. Then kertal confessed. The arm broke off while the monster posed for its career as an Instagram influencer, and the post office story was made up. The monster was promised a sturdier body with a steel skeleton. Its answer: I’ll be back (with 2 hands)! And to everyone else: Flakiness for all is my slogan!

    1 / 8

  4. Vienna

    Hasta la vista, flaky test

    In the meantime the monster’s world expanded: the Flakinator was sent from the future to help with the flakiness. Famous last words of a flaky test before it is quarantined: I’ll be unskipped!

  5. The fan club

    Everybody gets the eyes

    Brave flaky test hunters started wearing the monster’s big googly eyes as a disguise for the hunt.

  6. To sum it up

    Measures to resolve the flakiness

    Several measures were applied to get rid of the monster. All of them were flaky.

    • Relocation. Convince the monster to stay in Italy, Croatia, Austria, Spain or Las Vegas. It came back.
    • A new career. Open a LinkedIn account for it, with the Open to work badge. The account was blocked. No sense of humor on LinkedIn.
    • Spreading the load. Give away cute Flaky Test Monster prints and buttons to other teams and companies. No reduction in flakiness observed.

  7. October 2026

    Monster at Happylab

    On 9 October the goblin from the CI pipeline entered the physical world, as one of the makers’ exhibits at the 20th anniversary of the Happylab makerspace in Vienna. The monster refused to stay there, and the flakiness continued unabated.
