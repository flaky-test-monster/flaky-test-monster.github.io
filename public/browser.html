human →becomes 3D relief, in your browser

Take a photo or upload one. Free, open models run right here in your browser and turn it into a 3D-printable relief of you, smooth or built from LEGO-style bricks. No server, no API keys, and the photo never leaves this device. The first run downloads about 125 MB of models; later runs use the browser's cache.

The camera is blocked or missing. Upload a photo instead, or allow camera access and reload.
