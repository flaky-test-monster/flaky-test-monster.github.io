The Eurovision Monster Contest. Today is a good day for your ears to die.

Flaky on CI, flaky on stage. Let’s make the Eurovision Song Contest flaky. First the monster needs a country that’s honoured to nominate it, so here is a song for every candidate. Who will send a monster?

Songs

You are the jury. As at the Eurovision Song Contest, give 12 points to your favourite, then 10, 8, 7 and down to 1, right next to each song. Each score goes to one song only.
