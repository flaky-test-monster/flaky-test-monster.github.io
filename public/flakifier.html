      At Happylab

      Built for the makers' exhibition at the 20th anniversary of Happylab, Austria's first makerspace. The exhibit turned a 2D photo into a 3D-printable file in three ways: a pipeline of frontier models in the cloud, open models running locally on a Mac, and transformer models running right in the browser. In the end the simplest trick was the most popular: the webcam tracks your mouth, and the wider you open it, the harsher the picture gets, until it turns green, monster eyes appear and the camera takes your photo.

      A nice side effect: a new large-format printer in the lab, for prints up to A0, and a demo of an LLM translating a song into Mandarin, started the monster's next goal: music for a European Monster Song Contest.
