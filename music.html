The Eurovision Slop Song Slong Contest. Today is a good day for your ears to die.

3D printed at Happylab, flaky on CI, flaky on stage. Let’s make the Eurovision Slop Song Slong Contest flaky. First the monster needs a country that’s honoured to nominate it, so here is a song for every candidate. Who will send a monster?

Songs

You're the jury. Distribute the points. The winner might get an enhanced deslopification procedure.

  • The Flaky Test Monster · From Flaky With Love

    The international version

    In English. No risk, less fun. Suitable for the UK, Ireland, Texas, Utah, Canada… maybe it should be picked by Australia to ensure their first win. Great CI software comes from there too. A home match for the monster.

  • Το Τέρας των Ασταθών Τεστ · Από τον Flaky, με πάθος

    The Greek version

    Inspired by Greek heroes battling flaky tests: definitely more risk and more drama than the international version.

  • Windiges Test Monsta · Busserl vom Flaki

    The Austrian version

    With some “creativity” and knüppelharte translation work, it could also be Germany’s candidate. Or Switzerland’s. Or Britain’s: Look Mum No Computer’s recent song was partly in German too. 100% German, 100% risk, 100% fun.

  • Qapbe’bogh test Ha’DIbaH · Flaky vo’, batlh

    The Klingon version

    First the European Broadcasting Union needs a word with the High Council. Australia got in, so why not Qo’noS? Klingons respect a worthy foe, and no foe is more worthy than a test that fails one run in ten. Klingon is the only language where a failing test is a glorious death, not a bug, so the monster feels at home. Heghlu’meH QaQ jajvam: today is a good day to die. Tomorrow too, if you re-run the pipeline. Press play and the lights go down: Klingons don’t do parchment. Qapla’!

  • El Monstruo de los Tests Inestables · Desde Flaky, con salero

    The Spanish version

    ¡Olé! Spain is home to many fighters of flaky tests, and this song honours the monster’s foes with flair and passion.

  • Il Mostro dei Test Instabili · Da Flaky, con furore

    The Italian version

    Italy, playing it safe. Italy last won in 2021, so the monster plays copycat. A successful strategy can be repeated with minor tweaks. A test that was flaky once can have a second chance.

  • Potwór Niestabilnych Testów · Od Flaky’ego, z przytupem

    The Polish version

    Poland is home to Knitty McGritty, one of the monster’s foes. Honour your foes! And Poland never won so far. Wat?? Let the monster take care of it.

  • Le Monstre des Tests Instables · Et voilà, ton flaky test !

    The French version

    France last won in 1977. Time to go back to basics: grand emotion, a chanson. After all, flaky tests aren’t funny. They are a hidden tragedy.

  • Mrrrow Prrr-ow · Miau, mrrp!

    The cat version

    In cat, says a language model. Doubtful: ask a cat to sing and it walks off. Cats don’t sing; they order a human, or a monster, to sing for them.
